# LeadAIO Rebrand & Launch — Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Launch LeadAIO as a stronger, more credible enterprise brand and establish it as the category leader in AI Optimization (AIO), covering AIO, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization).

**Audience:** mid-market and enterprise marketing leaders (CMO, VP Digital, Head of SEO/Content).

**Timeline:** 16 weeks from kickoff, in four phases. Launch day is the end of **week 12**. Week numbers are relative to kickoff, and the real calendar date gets set in initiative 01.

The interactive version of this plan is in `index.html`.

---

## 6. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Brand sign-off slips | Moves launch day | Fixed decision date in week 4; limit to 2–3 directions; one approver |
| Customer approvals are slow | Thin proof at launch | Start week 1; offer anonymized studies as fallback; use our own AIO results (05) |
| Web dev capacity | Site not ready by week 11 | Lock scope in week 4; launch core pages first, industry pages after |
| Tracking gaps | Can't prove ROI | Tracking spec in week 4; end-to-end test in week 11 before spend |
| Paid spend underperforms | High cost per SQL | Small test budgets per segment first; scale only winners |
| AI visibility moves slowly | Weak north-star story early | Report leading indicators (citations, entity fixes, mentions) monthly |
